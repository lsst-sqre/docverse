### New features

- Added the `purgatory_cleanup` worker pair that actually reclaims expired build content. The daily dispatcher cron on the maintenance pool (03:23 UTC by default, `DOCVERSE_PURGATORY_CLEANUP_CRON_HOUR` / `_MINUTE`) checks `DOCVERSE_PURGATORY_CLEANUP_ENABLED` before touching the database — a disabled sweep writes no rows and enqueues no jobs — then creates one `purgatory_cleanup` queue job per organization holding a build past that organization's own retention. An organization whose per-org mutex is already held is stepped over rather than taking the rest of the fan-out down with it.
- The per-org job plans its work in one short read transaction, then reclaims each build with no transaction open and stamps `date_purged` in its own short transaction immediately after. The ordering is deliberate: a crash leaves objects gone and the row unstamped, which the next tick converges on, rather than a stamped row whose content no later tick would ever revisit. A build a live edition still serves is held back and logged at warning level with the edition slugs; a build restored between planning and the delete is skipped with its content intact; a build restored between the delete and the stamp is counted as failed and logged at error level, because that is the one outcome the sweep cannot repair itself. One build's failure never abandons the rest of the work list, so the job ends `completed` or `completed_with_errors` and the queue row's progress carries the counts, the byte total, whether the per-job cap ended the list, and the public ids of everything failed or skipped.
