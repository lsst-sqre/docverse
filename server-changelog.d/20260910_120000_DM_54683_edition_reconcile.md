### New features

- A periodic `edition_reconcile` loop now finds editions whose recorded publish state has drifted from what the CDN actually serves and converges them. A per-org job, dispatched half-hourly on the maintenance pool, compares each edition's current build against the `edition_build_history` pair and against the Cloudflare Workers KV pointer read back in bulk, then re-drives a lost or stalled publish (as an ordinary `publish_edition` enqueue tagged `trigger=reconcile`) and deletes the key of an edition that has been tombstoned. Pairs reading `failed` are reported and left for operators, and an organization with no CDN service runs the database leg alone. Three knobs shape it: `edition_reconcile_enabled`, `edition_reconcile_max_actions_per_job`, and `edition_reconcile_reaper_threshold_seconds`.
- Every tick publishes an org-scoped `edition_reconcile_completed` metrics event and records its tally on the job's `queue_jobs.progress`, whether or not it repaired anything; a tick that did repair something also logs a warning naming the editions and sends one Sentry message for the organization.

### Other changes

- `docs/edition-reconcile.md` is a new operations page for the loop: the drift it detects, its full decision table, the config knobs and their Phalanx values, the cron minute slots, and how to read one tick's outcome, including how to re-drive a `failed` pair by hand. `docs/index.md` is a new index over the `docs/` tree.
- The `publish_edition_reaper_threshold_seconds` docstring no longer implies the reap clears a `publishing` pair. It never did; the pair is exactly the signal `edition_reconcile` reads to re-drive the publish.
