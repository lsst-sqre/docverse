### Other changes

- The operations docs now describe lifecycle deletions as reversible by activity and an operator's delete as permanent (PRD #830). `docs/github-integration.md` gains a "Lifecycle deletions come back with activity" section: the two records of why an edition was deleted (`editions.delete_reason` and the keeper-sync tombstone with its `tombstone_note` rule type), how a client build for a lifecycle-deleted edition's ref revives it while a `manual_delete` slug stays reserved, how keeper-sync judges a lifecycle tombstone again when LTD rebuilds the edition and what the cleared, kept, and refs-unavailable verdicts do, the admin `POST /orgs/{org}/projects/{project}/editions/{edition}/restore`, and a table of the log lines each path writes. `docs/keeper-sync-scope.md` gains a "Tombstones" section listing each tombstone reason with what writes and what lifts it, the clear-then-refresh recipe (`DELETE /orgs/{org}/keeper-sync/tombstones/{tombstone}` then `POST /orgs/{org}/keeper-sync/projects/{ltd_slug}/refresh`) used by hand on 2026-10-21 and when it is still needed, and the edition restore for an edition with a Docverse row. `docs/metrics.md` names the three `edition_lifecycle` `restore` emitters (the restore handler, `build_processing`, and `keeper_sync_project`), and `docs/api-conventions.md` gains a "Soft-deleted editions and builds" section on the two `restore` routes and `delete_reason`, with the `KeeperSyncTombstoneReason` example brought up to date. `tests/docs_test.py` pins the new sections to the routes, enums, and log lines in the code. Module and function docstrings in the keeper-sync tombstone service and worker no longer describe every tombstone as a permanent veto.
