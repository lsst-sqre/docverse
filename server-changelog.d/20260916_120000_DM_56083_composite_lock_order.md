### Bug fixes

- Closed the last two ways `DELETE /orgs/{org}/projects/{project}` could deadlock against a concurrent repoint. The `projects -> editions -> builds` lock order is a property of a whole transaction, not of one `EditionStore.set_current_build` call, and two writers surrounded a repoint with other row writes: keeper-sync's synced-build finalize stamped the build's content hash, inventory, and status before repointing the edition at it, and upload-completion tracking repointed a build's other matching editions before reaching `__main` (which sorts last under the database collation). Both now take the project and edition rows through the new `EditionStore.lock_for_repoint` at the head of their transaction, so a project delete landing mid-sync or mid-upload makes one side wait instead of aborting both. Edition pointers, build history, publish jobs, and the project clock are unaffected.
