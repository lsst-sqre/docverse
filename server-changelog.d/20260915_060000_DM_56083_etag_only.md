### Bug fixes

- The single project's `ETag` hashes the organization, project, and default-edition clocks as three separate parts rather than their maximum. `max()` reintroduced exactly the non-monotonicity the listing's watermark had already been fixed for: `date_updated` is PostgreSQL's transaction *start* time, so a `project_github_resolve` or keeper-sync `__main` repoint that begins early and waits on a row lock commits a clock below the organization's, the maximum does not move, and a poller holding the tag is told `304 Not Modified` until some unrelated write exceeds it. Any one of the three clocks moving — in either direction — now retires the tag.

### Other changes

- The project-listing watermark is the organization's project count and the sum of every project's `date_updated`; the newest clock is gone, since it existed only to name an instant and the two aggregates beside it already covered every insert, update, and soft delete regardless of commit order. `ProjectStore.get_org_watermark` no longer takes an `empty_fallback`: an organization owning no projects aggregates to zeroes, and the endpoint's tag hashes its public id anyway, so two empty organizations still validate apart.
