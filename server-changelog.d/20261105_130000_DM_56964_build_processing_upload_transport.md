### Bug fixes

- `build_processing` now uploads a build's files over the worker's dedicated copy client, under the same worker-wide upload cap as keeper-sync's build copies (`keeper_sync_upload_concurrency`, 32 by default), instead of over the worker's shared HTTP client 50 files at a time. On roundtable-dev a pipelines daily of about 8,600 files failed in 40 s, every one of its first 50 presigned PUTs timing out connecting to R2: the shared client's 5 s connect timeout and 20 kept-alive connections could not carry the burst from a busy pod. The copy client gives each attempt a 10 s connect window and keeps a connection alive for every slot of the cap, so a large build reuses its connections to R2. Each job also fans its uploads out no wider than the cap, and every `build_processing` job in one worker process shares it. Uploads keep the shared per-object retry budget, since the job uploads inside the transaction that completes the build. `docs/build-processing.md` documents the shared transport under "Upload transport".
