### Bug fixes

- Recording the outcome of a dashboard-template sync no longer moves the binding's `date_updated`. `DashboardGitHubTemplateBindingStore.update_sync_state` — the writer that runs on every sync success and every sync failure — assigned ORM attributes and so let `onupdate` stamp the clock, while its seven sibling GitHub-side writers pinned it. That made `date_updated` on `GET /orgs/{org}/dashboard-template` read as "when a sync last ran" rather than "when an operator last edited the source coordinates", which is what the column means everywhere else.
