### Bug fixes

- The keeper-sync worker no longer opens up to a hundred connections to LTD Keeper at once. Each edition listing keeps at most 8 GETs in flight, but every LTD call went over the HTTP client the worker shares with discovery, GitHub, Cloudflare KV and CDN purges (100 connections), so the tier crons plus up to `DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_MAX_JOBS` concurrent sync jobs together could hold about a hundred GETs, and open about a hundred new connections, against the one LTD Keeper deployment. On roundtable-prod on 2026-10-08, after 1.0.0b8, every hourly `tier_other` tick logged hundreds of LTD `ConnectTimeout` retries, and 3 to 6 `keeper_sync_project` jobs per tick failed with `LtdClientError` once their retries ran out. LTD API calls in the worker now go over a dedicated client capped at 16 connections, all kept alive for 60 s so a burst reuses them instead of dialing new ones. A call past the cap waits for a free connection rather than failing on a pool timeout. Connect, read and write timeouts stay at 5 s, `LtdClient`'s retries are unchanged, and the cap is a fixed constant, not a setting. The API process is unaffected.
