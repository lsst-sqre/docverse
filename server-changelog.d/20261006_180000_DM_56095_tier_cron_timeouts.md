### Bug fixes

- The keeper-sync tier crons now run under arq timeouts derived from their own cron intervals instead of arq's 300 s default: 240 s for `keeper_sync_tier_main` (every 5 minutes), 1500 s for `keeper_sync_tier_discovery` (every 30 minutes), and 3000 s for `keeper_sync_tier_other` (hourly). Each is the interval less a sixth of it, and at least a minute less, so arq cancels a pass before the same tier's next tick fires and two passes of one tier never overlap. On roundtable-prod on 2026-10-06, after `pipelines` (2,938 LTD editions) entered the `rubin` organization's scope, the discovery and other passes reached the 300 s default on every tick, and the projects at the tail of the scope were never visited. A cancelled pass now logs one `Keeper-sync tier pass cancelled` warning with the tier, the inferred `reason` (`job_timeout` or `worker_shutdown`), how long it ran, the organizations it finished, and how far it got through the in-flight organization's scope. A project job the pass was handing to arq when it was cancelled is failed with its cancellation read against the tier's timeout rather than 300 s. The timeouts are documented in the keeper-sync time budget page.
