### New features

- Keeper-sync build-content copies now give each presigned upload to R2 its own retry budget instead of the shared four-attempt, 10 s-ceiling default, so a copy outlasts a multi-second R2 connect outage rather than failing the edition. Two new settings control it: `DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_UPLOAD_MAX_ATTEMPTS` (default 6, at least 1) and `DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_UPLOAD_MAX_BACKOFF_SECONDS` (default 30.0, at least 0). Six attempts sleep 0.5 + 1 + 2 + 4 + 8 s between them, which with a 10 s connect timeout lets an object ride out about 65 s of outage; the 30 s ceiling also lets R2's `Retry-After` through up to 30 s instead of clamping it to 10 s. Only the keeper-sync worker's copy destination uses the new budget; the API process and every other worker path keep the shared defaults.
