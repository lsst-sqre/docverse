### Bug fixes

- Listing an LTD product's editions no longer follows the edition URLs one at a time. The legacy LTD API has no bulk edition endpoint, so `LtdClient.list_editions_for_product` makes one GET per edition, and on roundtable-prod on 2026-10-06, after `pipelines` (2,938 LTD editions) entered the `rubin` organization's scope, that one call took about two minutes. It is made at the start of every `keeper_sync_project` slice that has no resume cursor, and by the keeper-sync discovery and other tier crons once per in-scope project per tick, which pushed both crons past arq's 300 s cron timeout on every tick. The edition GETs now run concurrently, at most 8 at once (a fixed constant that bounds the load on LTD, not a setting), and the editions are still returned in the order LTD lists them. Each GET keeps the same retries as before. The first GET to fail cancels the others, and its `LtdNotFoundError` or `LtdClientError` is raised unchanged, as the one-at-a-time walk did.
