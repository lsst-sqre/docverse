### Bug fixes

- The keeper-sync worker now copies build content over a dedicated HTTP client instead of the one shared with discovery, the LTD API, GitHub, Cloudflare KV and CDN purges. The copy client connects with a 10 s timeout (the shared client's is 5 s), allows 60 s to send an object and read R2's reply, and waits up to 30 s for a free connection. Its pool holds `DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_MAX_JOBS` × `DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_COPY_CONCURRENCY` + 10 connections (90 at the defaults), so no presigned upload waits on the pool at full copy concurrency. Before, the stock 80 concurrent uploads ran the shared 100-connection pool near its ceiling, and a wait for a pooled connection surfaced as an upload timeout. The timeouts are fixed in code, not configurable.
