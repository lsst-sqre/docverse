### Other changes

- The keeper-sync stuck-run reaper now waits 90 minutes rather than 6 hours by default. `DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_REAPER_THRESHOLD_SECONDS` no longer carries an independent 21600 s literal: when it is unset the threshold derives from `DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_JOB_TIMEOUT_SECONDS` plus a 30-minute margin (`KEEPER_SYNC_REAPER_MARGIN_SECONDS`), giving 5400 s at stock settings. The keeper-sync arq functions run with `max_tries=1`, so arq has already cancelled any job that reached its 1 h timeout — a child `queue_jobs` row still `in_progress` past that point is definitively dead, and the extra five hours only kept the project parked behind the partial unique index that the tier cron reads as an active job. An OOM-killed sync worker on roundtable-dev held `documenteer` and `safir` out of the tier rotation for the full six hours this way. Driving the job timeout down in test/staging now drags the reaper down with it instead of leaving a 6 h floor, and setting the threshold env var explicitly still overrides the derivation. The margin stays at one full `keeper_sync_reaper` cron gap (the job runs on `cron(minute={0, 30})`), so worst-case detection latency is unchanged in character. The other five reaper thresholds keep their literal defaults.
