### Bug fixes

- A build soft-deleted while its edition pointer was in flight no longer becomes the edition an organization serves. `EditionStore.set_current_build` now refuses a target whose `date_deleted` is set — reported the same way as the stale-build guard, so edition tracking records the same skip — and the `publish_edition` worker re-reads the build under its `EDITION_UPDATE` lock, writing no CDN pointer and completing the job with `deleted_skipped` in its progress when the build went away between tracking and publish. Without both guards, a DELETE landing in that window left an edition resolving to content the purgatory sweep is entitled to reclaim once the organization's retention elapses.
