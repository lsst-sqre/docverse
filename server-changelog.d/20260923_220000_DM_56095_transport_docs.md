### Other changes

- `docs/keeper-sync-transport.md` is a new operations page for keeper-sync transport resilience: the three retry settings (`DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_UPLOAD_MAX_ATTEMPTS`, `DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_UPLOAD_MAX_BACKOFF_SECONDS`, `DOCVERSE_KEEPER_SYNC_COPY_RETRY_DELAY_SECONDS`) with their defaults, and why Phalanx values for them are optional; the ride-out arithmetic (about 65 s per object, just under three minutes per copy with the build-level retry); the copy client's fixed timeouts and how its connection pool is derived from `keeper_sync_max_jobs` × `keeper_sync_copy_concurrency`; what the build-level retry does and does not re-run; what happens to an edition that still fails; and how to read a `build_content_copied` event, including one with `exhausted_object_count` above zero. It is linked from `docs/index.md` and from the keeper-sync scope page's related reading, and `tests/docs_test.py` fails when the page stops matching the settings, the copy-client constants, the event's fields or the ride-out arithmetic in the code.
