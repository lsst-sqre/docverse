### Bug fixes

- Editions in a tracking mode that only ever matches release refs — `eups_weekly_release`, `eups_daily_release` and `eups_release` — now derive the `release` kind. Keeper-sync imports LTD's `eups_weekly_release` and `eups_daily_release` editions as releases (replacing the old `eups_daily_release` → `draft` pin), so pipelines' `weekly` and `daily` meta editions, whose own slugs match no version rule, stop importing as drafts that age out under `draft_inactivity`. Existing derived-kind drafts converge to `release` on the next keeper-sync poll or on the next build they track (logged as `Converged edition kind` with `kind_trigger="build_upload"` and the edition's `tracking_mode`). A `declared` kind is never rewritten, `__main` keeps its `main` kind in any tracking mode, the per-day `d_*` editions stay drafts, and `lsst_doc` and `eups_major_release` keep their ref-confirmed kind.
