### Bug fixes

- `lifecycle_reaper` now sweeps its two families for abandoned queue jobs — a `queue_jobs` row that reached `status='queued'` with a non-null `backend_job_id` and was then lost by arq, which neither the silent pass (`in_progress` only) nor the orphan pass (`backend_job_id IS NULL` only) could see. `QueueJobStore.fail_abandoned_lifecycle_eval_jobs` and `QueueJobStore.fail_abandoned_git_ref_audit_jobs` age candidates out on `DOCVERSE_LIFECYCLE_REAPER_THRESHOLD_SECONDS` (6 h at stock settings) and fail a row only after `QueueBackend.get_job_metadata` confirms arq has no record of it, so a job merely backed up behind a saturated maintenance pool is never cancelled. Reaping releases the per-org mutex the wedged row held — `idx_queue_jobs_lifecycle_eval_active_uq` or `idx_queue_jobs_git_ref_audit_active_uq` — so the next dispatcher or discovery tick enqueues fresh work for that org instead of skipping it forever, and reaped rows still roll their parent `lifecycle_eval_runs` / `git_ref_audit_runs` row up through the same finalisation pass. Reaped rows carry `errors.type='AbandonedQueueJob'`, and the reaper's warning line gains `lifecycle_eval_abandoned_count`, `git_ref_audit_abandoned_count`, and a `reaped_jobs` list naming the matching sweep and `backend_job_id` for every row it failed. An unreachable queue backend aborts only the two abandoned passes for that tick.
